How it works

Join our Discord

Use the button above. It's free.

Press Apply

In the #apply channel, press Apply and answer a few questions.

Chat with staff

You get a private channel with our staff. They may ask you a thing or two there.

Start flying

Once approved, we email your Pilot ID and login. Download AloCARS and book your first flight.

Before you apply

You'll need
  • A VATSIM account (your CID is checked)
  • MSFS 2020 or 2024, or X-Plane 11 or 12
  • A Windows PC for AloCARS, our flight tracker
  • A Discord account and an email address
We'll ask
  • Your name and email
  • Your VATSIM CID
  • Your home base and simulator
  • A little about your flight sim experience

Questions

How long does approval take?

Usually a day or two. Staff reply in your private channel, and you'll get a Discord notification when they do.

Where's my login?

When you're approved, we email your Pilot ID and password, and the bot posts your Pilot ID in your channel. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't show up.

Can I check on my application?

Yes. In #apply, press Check my application any time.

Already a pilot?

Download AloCARS and sign in with your Pilot ID or email.
