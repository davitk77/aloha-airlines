Names on the nose

How our aircraft are named

Our 737s

Each 737 is named for a Hawaiian mountain, from Hāʻupu on Kauaʻi to Mauna Kea on Hawaiʻi Island, the highest point in the state at 13,803 ft.

Our 757s

The 757s honour Hawaiian royalty: King Kamehameha I, who united the islands in 1810, and Queen Liliʻuokalani, the last monarch of the Hawaiian Kingdom.

Do I need real-world flying experience?

No. Ask in Discord and someone will help you through your first sector.

Which simulators can I use?

Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 or 2024, and X-Plane 11 or 12. You'll need a 737 or 757 that matches the flight you book.

Do I have to fly online?

No. VATSIM is optional. Flying online with AloCARS running adds live ATC and other pilots for a more immersive flight, and earns 50 bonus points. IVAO counts too.

About us

A virtual airline with island roots

We fly the Spirit of Aloha: a Hawaiʻi airline run by and for flight simmers.

Our Mission

Aloha Airlines is a premium virtual airline based out of Kahului, Hawaii, simulating scheduled air carrier operations in both the Hawaiian Islands and the U.S mainland. Our mission – encapsulated in our slogan, “The way flying was meant to be” – is to provide each passenger with an incredible flying experience, from the check-in counter to the baggage claim. While we don’t claim to be the cheapest, we promise to deliver an experience that makes every journey worthwhile.

Rooted in the Spirit of Aloha, we take pride in bringing back the little things that once made air travel special: genuine hospitality, attentive service, generous amenities on and off the aircraft, and an unwavering commitment to our passengers. At Aloha, we believe that flying shouldn’t be an obstacle between you and an incredible vacation away, but rather a highlight.

We believe there’s a better way to fly and we’d love to show you.

Our Operations

Behind every Aloha Airlines flight is a fully developed airline operation. We believe that rather than just a fun livery and a few interesting routes, pilots want an immersive virtual airline experience that goes beyond simply taking off and landing. That’s why we’re building Aloha around the systems, standards, and structure of a real-world Part 121 air carrier.

From initial pilot training and aircraft qualifications to recurrent training requirements, standardized operating procedures, custom checklists, and comprehensive flight operations manuals, our operation is being designed to reflect the professionalism and organization of our real-world counterparts. At Aloha, every pilot has a role to play, and every flight is part of something much, much bigger.

With that, we also recognize our pilots fly because they enjoy it. We believe realism should enhance, not encumber, the simulation experience. Our procedures and requirements are designed to strike a practical balance between authentic airline operations and the realities of everyday life. Whether you have time for several flights a week or just one flight a month, we want Aloha to be an airline you can genuinely enjoy being a part of.

What truly sets our operation apart, however, is what happens behind the scenes. Every flight flown for Aloha is individually modeled and incorporated into our airline’s economic simulation. Flights aren’t just logged and forgotten; they contribute to the financial performance, operational development, and continued growth of the company. Aircraft have operating costs and maintenance requirements, routes generate revenue, and every operational decision has consequences. The airline grows with you.

Our Legacy

We would be remiss if not to mention the shoulders we stand on. Aloha Airlines is named after a defunct real-world airline of the same name. Founded in 1946 as Trans-Pacific Airlines, the original Aloha Airlines began with a simple mission: to connect the people of Hawaii through accessible, dependable air travel. Renamed Aloha Airlines in 1958, the carrier grew to become an enduring fixture of Hawaiian aviation, earning a special place in the communities it served through its distinctive identity, familiar Boeing aircraft, and genuine spirit of island hospitality.

Unfortunately, after more than six decades in the skies, Aloha Airlines brought its passenger operations to a close in March 2008. While its aircraft eventually departed and its operations fell silent, the legacy it left behind remains an important part of Hawaii’s aviation history.

Today, Aloha Airlines returns to the virtual skies, carrying the legacy forward. Inspired by the original carrier’s history, traditions, and commitment to passenger service, we seek to recapture the experience that made Aloha special while building a virtual airline of our own. Through a meticulously modeled operation, a modern fleet, and an unwavering commitment to the Spirit of Aloha, we honor the past while charting our own course into the future.
